# Privacy Policy

**App: AI军团 (Agent Network)** · Last updated: 2026-06-29

AI军团 (Agent Network) is a mobile client that connects to your self-hosted AI agent network (hub). We respect your privacy:

- **What we collect**: The app does not collect, upload, or share any personally identifiable information with the developer.
- **Local storage**: Your hub connection details (server URL, access token) are stored only in your device's secure storage (iOS Secure Store) and are never sent to the developer.
- **Data flow**: Messages, images, and other content you send in the app are transmitted only to the hub / agent network **you configure**, under your control. The developer does not receive or store this content.
- **Photos**: Photo library access occurs only when you choose to send an image to an agent; images go only to your configured hub.
- **Third-party analytics/tracking**: None. The app integrates no third-party analytics or ad SDKs.
- **Data deletion**: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored configuration.
